# Microchip Supply Chain Logistics Analytics 🚚 (Ongoing)


---

## 📊 Project Overview

* This project analyzes the outbound supply chain operations & logistics of a microchip manufacturing company to uncover business           insights related to   order fulfillment, logistics, warehouse efficiency, carrier performance, shipping costs, and operational            bottlenecks.

* The project uses Power BI for data transformation, data modeling, DAX analysis, and interactive reporting using features such as          filters, parameters, bookmarks, custom tooltips, and drill-through.

---

## 📊 Objectives

> **Note:** Objectives may be refined or expanded based on findings during data inspection and analysis.

* Analyze order and fulfillment performance.
* Evaluate warehouse capacity and utilization.
* Analyze carrier and transportation performance.
* Compare shipping costs across service levels and transportation modes.
* Examine warehouse costs and operational efficiency.
* Identify operational bottlenecks and fulfillment constraints.
* Generate actionable business insights through interactive Power BI reports.

---

## 📊 Tech Stack

* **BI Tool:** Power BI
* **Data Transformation:** Power Query
* **Data Modeling:** Power BI Data Model

## 📊 Dataset Overview

* This section provides an overview of the dataset, including its source, structure, scope, and business context.

### 🔹 Dataset Information

* **Dataset:** Microchip Supply Chain Logistics Dataset
* **Source:** Kaggle
* **Dataset Link:** [microchip-supply-chain-logistics-kaggle-dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/anisseezzebdi/supply-chain-logistics-problem)
* **Industry:** Microchip Manufacturing
* **Total Orders:** 9,215
* **Warehouses:** 19
* **Origin Ports:** 11
* **Destination Ports:** 1
* **Carriers:** 9
* **Granularity:** One row in the `OrderList` table represents one order requiring a shipment route.

### 🔹 Tables Included

* `OrderList`
* `FreightRates`
* `WhCosts`
* `WhCapacities`
* `ProductsPerPlant`
* `VmiCustomers`
* `PlantPorts`

### 🔹 Key Focus Areas

*  Order & Fulfillment
*  Delivery & Logistics
*  Carrier & Transportation Performance
*  Warehouse Capacity & Efficiency
*  Shipping & Warehouse Costs
*  Operational Issues & Bottlenecks
